PRIVACY POLICY

NekoKan: Cat Inspector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

日本語

This policy describes how user information is handled in the iOS app “NekoKan: Cat Inspector” (Japanese title: ねこ鑑定士 ねこかん; the “App”). The App is provided by Takamune Aoki (the “Developer”).

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer does not collect any information about you. The Developer has no servers, and there is no account registration. The Developer does not receive your name, email address, location, or similar information.

However, because the App shows ads, Google, which delivers the ads, may obtain information (see Section 4).

2. Information stored on your device

Your Field Guide, the rankings on this device, and the App's settings (such as sound effects on/off and whether you have finished the tutorial) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the Developer. Because the Developer keeps no copy of your records, the Developer cannot restore them if they are lost when you delete the App or change devices.

When you use “Share”, the share image is created on your device and is sent only to the app or service you choose in the iOS share sheet.

3. Communication with outside services

The App does not communicate with any server run by the Developer. However, it uses Google's services for Section 4 and Apple's services for Sections 5 and 6, and communicates with each of them.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using “Google AdMob” by Google LLC. It shows the following two kinds of ads. A banner ad at the bottom of the screen is always shown, including while you are playing. A rewarded video ad is shown only when you choose to watch it in order to continue a game, once per game.

To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may send the following information to Google:

  • IP address (used to estimate your approximate region)
  • Performance information such as crash logs and how fast the App runs
  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and identifiers assigned per app or per developer
  • Advertising information (such as which ads were shown)
  • Interaction information (such as ad taps and video views)

This information is handled by Google; the Developer does not receive it. It is handled in accordance with Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

Permission to track (App Tracking Transparency)

When you first launch the App, it asks for your permission to track using Apple's system prompt. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is sent to Google only if you allow it. If you do not allow it, none of the App's features are restricted. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, the App asks for your consent to the handling of your information on Google's consent screen before showing ads. You can change your choices later in the App under Settings > “Privacy Choices”.

5. In-app purchase

“Remove Ads” (a one-time purchase) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the purchase and manages your payment information; the Developer does not receive your payment information.

After you buy “Remove Ads”, banner ads are no longer shown, and you can continue without watching an ad. If you reinstall the App, you can bring back your purchase with “Restore Purchase” in Settings.

6. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App sends your score for the world rankings and your achievement progress to Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center nickname is shown in the rankings. This information is managed by Apple; the Developer does not receive it. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

7. Sharing with third parties

Because the Developer does not receive your information, the Developer does not provide it to any third party. Note that the information involved in delivering ads, described in Section 4, is sent directly from the App to Google.

8. Deleting information and stopping transmission

  • The Field Guide and ranking records stored on your device are erased when you delete the App.
  • To stop sending information to Game Center, sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings. Deleting records already sent to Game Center is done through Apple's procedures.
  • To stop sending the advertising identifier (IDFA) to Google, do not allow tracking, as described in Section 4.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland can change their ad consent at any time under Settings > “Privacy Choices” in the App.
  • The record of your “Remove Ads” purchase is managed by Apple. Even after reinstalling the App, you can bring it back with “Restore Purchase”.

9. Reporting inappropriate ads

If an inappropriate ad appears in the App, please let us know at the contact address in Section 12. It helps if you include what the ad showed, when you saw it, and on which screen.

10. Children

The App does not collect information targeted at any particular age group.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
