Cat or Not?: Cat Inspector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

日本語

This policy describes how user information is handled in the iOS app “Cat or Not?: Cat Inspector” (Japanese title: ねこ鑑定士 ねこかん; the “App”). The App is provided by Takamune Aoki (the “Developer”).

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer has no servers, and there is no account registration. The Developer does not receive information that identifies you personally, such as your name, email address, or precise location.

However, to improve the App, the App collects usage information using Google's “Firebase Analytics” (see Section 3). Also, because the App shows ads, Google, which delivers the ads, may obtain information (see Section 5).

2. Information stored on your device and in iCloud

Your Field Guide, the rankings and Daily Challenge records on this device, and the App's settings (such as sound effects on/off and whether you have finished the tutorial) are stored on your device. They are never sent to the Developer.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your Field Guide, your rankings and Daily Challenge records, and whether you have seen the tutorial and the Field Guide completion celebration are also saved to your own iCloud through Apple's iCloud (key-value storage), so that your records carry over between devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Records saved in iCloud are managed by Apple; the Developer does not receive them. Apple's handling of them is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Your sound setting and your ad-related choices are not saved to iCloud.

Because the Developer keeps no copy of your records, the Developer cannot restore them if they are lost when you delete the App or change devices (for example, when you do not use iCloud).

When you use “Share”, the share image is created on your device and is sent only to the app or service you choose in the iOS share sheet.

3. Usage information (Firebase Analytics)

To learn where players stop playing and how difficult the App feels, and to make it easier and more enjoyable to play, the App uses “Firebase Analytics” (Google Analytics) provided by Google LLC.

  • Information collected
    • Records of how the App is used: app launches and time spent, showing and finishing the first-time “How to Play”, game starts and results (normal game or Daily Challenge, number of inspections, how the game ended, speed-up stage, whether and how you continued), Game Center achievements unlocked, which characters' Field Guide entries you opened, the kind of share image you created, and in-app purchases (item and price)
    • Device and app information: device model, OS version, app version, and language and region settings
    • An identifier that Firebase randomly assigns to each installation of the App (app instance ID)
    • Approximate region estimated from your IP address (around the country or city level; Google does not use the device's Location Services)
  • Information not collected: the advertising identifier (IDFA) and IDFV, your name or contact details, your Game Center nickname, your Field Guide and records themselves, and the images you share
  • Purpose: limited to statistics for improving the App (finding where players stop, adjusting difficulty, and finding bugs). The Developer does not use this information to personalize ads or to identify you personally. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, use for advertising purposes (ad user data and ad personalization) is set to be denied unless you consent
  • Your consent choices: If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the choices you make on Google's consent screen (Section 5) also apply to this usage information
  • Retention: Data is deleted automatically after the period set in Google Analytics' data retention setting (14 months)
  • The Developer sees statistics aggregated by Google Analytics. The collected information is processed on Google's servers (including outside Japan) and handled in accordance with Google's policies:
    • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
    • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Communication with outside services

The App does not communicate with any server run by the Developer. However, it uses Google's services for Sections 3 and 5 and Apple's services for iCloud (Section 2) and Sections 6 and 7, and communicates with each of them.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using “Google AdMob” by Google LLC. It shows the following two kinds of ads. A banner ad at the bottom of the screen is always shown, including while you are playing. A rewarded video ad is shown only when you choose to watch it in order to continue a game, once per game.

The App is set to show only ads with content suitable for all ages (Google's “G” rating).

To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may send the following information to Google:

  • IP address (used to estimate your approximate region)
  • Performance information such as crash logs and how fast the App runs
  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and identifiers assigned per app or per developer
  • Advertising information (such as which ads were shown)
  • Interaction information (such as ad taps and video views)

This information is handled by Google; the Developer does not receive it. It is handled in accordance with Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

Permission to track (App Tracking Transparency)

When you first launch the App, it asks for your permission to track using Apple's system prompt. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is sent to Google only if you allow it. If you do not allow it, none of the App's features are restricted. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, the App asks for your consent to the handling of your information on Google's consent screen before showing ads. You can change your choices later in the App under Settings > “Privacy Choices”.

6. In-app purchase

“Remove Ads” (a one-time purchase) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the purchase and manages your payment information; the Developer does not receive your payment information.

After you buy “Remove Ads”, banner ads are no longer shown, and you can continue without watching an ad. If you reinstall the App, you can bring back your purchase with “Restore Purchase” in Settings.

7. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App sends your score for the world rankings and your achievement progress to Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center nickname is shown in the rankings. This information is managed by Apple; the Developer does not receive it. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

8. Sharing with third parties

The Developer does not provide your information to any third party. Note that the usage information described in Section 3 and the information involved in delivering ads described in Section 5 are sent directly from the App to Google.

9. Deleting information and stopping transmission

  • You can erase your records with “Delete My Data” under Settings in the App. This erases the Field Guide, ranking, and Daily Challenge records on this device, whether you have seen the tutorial and similar screens, and the records saved in iCloud (records on your other devices using the same Apple Account are erased when those devices connect to iCloud). If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievement progress is also reset to zero. The usage data kept on the device (Section 3) is also cleared and a new app instance ID is used (information already sent is deleted after the retention period). Erased records cannot be restored.
    Scores already sent to the Game Center world rankings cannot be deleted from the App (they are managed by Apple). Your “Remove Ads” purchase and the choices you made in “Privacy Choices” are not erased.
  • Records stored on your device are also erased when you delete the App. However, records saved in iCloud remain and come back if you reinstall the App. To erase the iCloud copy as well, use “Delete My Data” before deleting the App.
  • To stop saving to iCloud, sign out of iCloud in iOS Settings.
  • To stop sending information to Game Center, sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings. Deleting records already sent to Game Center (world ranking scores) is done through Apple's procedures.
  • To stop sending the advertising identifier (IDFA) to Google, do not allow tracking, as described in Section 5.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland can change their consent for ads and usage information at any time under Settings > “Privacy Choices” in the App.
  • There is no setting in the App to stop the usage information collection described in Section 3. If you do not want it to be sent, please delete the App. The app instance ID is no longer used once the App is deleted, and information collected until then is deleted after the retention period (14 months).
  • The record of your “Remove Ads” purchase is managed by Apple. Even after reinstalling the App, you can bring it back with “Restore Purchase”.

10. Reporting inappropriate ads

If an inappropriate ad appears in the App, please let us know at the contact address in Section 13. It helps if you include what the ad showed, when you saw it, and on which screen.

11. Children

The App does not collect information targeted at any particular age group.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
