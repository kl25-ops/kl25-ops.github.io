FAQ

How do I play?

Characters line up in a queue. Swipe the one at the front right if it is a cat, left if it is not. The game ends if you make a mistake or run out of time. You start with 4 seconds per character, and every 10 correct answers the game speeds up (“Speed Up!”). Your score is the number of characters you judged correctly. Everyone you judge correctly goes into the Field Guide (45 characters in all), and your scores are kept in the rankings. A short tutorial is shown the first time you play.

How do I get rid of ads?

Buy “Remove Ads” (a one-time purchase; 300 yen in Japan, local prices vary by country or region). Banner ads will no longer appear, and you can continue without watching an ad.

I bought “Remove Ads”, but I still see ads

Please try “Restore Purchase” in Settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Restoring is free of charge.

My records disappeared after I changed devices or reinstalled the app

Your Field Guide, rankings, and Daily Challenge records are stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on your new device, your records carry over when you open the app (it may take a little while for them to arrive). If you were not signed in to iCloud, we cannot restore your records on our side, because we do not run a server. A “Remove Ads” purchase can be brought back with “Restore Purchase”.

I want to erase my records and start over

Tap “Delete My Data” in the app's Settings. Your Field Guide, rankings, Daily Challenge records, and so on are erased, and the app starts fresh as if you were playing for the first time. Records saved in iCloud are erased too (on your other devices with the same Apple Account, they are erased when those devices connect to iCloud). If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements are also reset to zero. Erased records cannot be restored.
Scores already sent to the Game Center world rankings cannot be deleted from the app (they are managed by Apple). Your “Remove Ads” purchase and your “Privacy Choices” are not erased. Deleting the app removes the records on the device, but the iCloud copy remains.

My scores or achievements do not show up in Game Center

Please sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app. Scores and achievements earned while you are signed in are sent to the Game Center world rankings and achievements.

I saw an inappropriate ad

We are sorry. Please let us know at the contact address above. It helps us investigate if you include what the ad showed, when you saw it, and on which screen (and a screenshot, if you have one).
